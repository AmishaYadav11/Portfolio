Brand Strategy Case Study: Country Delight

Academic work | Independent analysis, not client work

Context

An academic case study analysing Country Delight with a focus on brand positioning, segmentation, and product versus brand differentiation.

My approach

I used segmentation, targeting and positioning to map the brand's intended customer, followed by a competitor comparison to understand its differentiation and communication.

Evidence and recommendations

The analysis recommends strengthening the brand's trust-led communication, making the value proposition more visible across customer touchpoints and using customer insights to improve retention.

Learning

This study taught me that brands differentiate not only through their product, but also through a clear promise, consistent experience and a strong understanding of customer needs.
