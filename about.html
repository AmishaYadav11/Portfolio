About Me

My Story

I grew up and studied in Ayodhya, completing my schooling at Udaya Public School and my B.Com at KS Saket P.G. College (Dr. Rammanohar Lohia Avadh University). I am now pursuing my MBA in Marketing & Business Analytics at IILM University, Greater Noida (2025-2027).

Coming from commerce, I wanted to add technical and creative skills to my foundation. That led me to Excel dashboards, Power BI and Tableau, which I have been learning through projects and certifications.

My interest in marketing grew from understanding why people choose one product or brand over another. Analytics helps me support those observations with data, identify useful patterns and communicate insights clearly.
